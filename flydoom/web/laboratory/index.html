What is actually trained?

Positive magnitude gains multiply a selected mask of existing fly synapses. Topology, signs, unselected edges, Laya and the engineered readout remain fixed. Gains are bounded to 0.75–1.25. Live play does not run an optimizer.

Interpretation limits

The input bridge bypasses the retina. Dynamics and neurotransmitter signs are model assumptions. This is an initial task-optimization pilot, not biological validation or a demonstration of general Doom navigation. Backward is enabled but useful retreat is not established.

Anatomical coverage

All 139,255 neuron anchors and 75 region surfaces are available. Up to 16 real neuron skeletons can be layered at once. This view does not contain every full neuronal arbor, EM volume, or individual synapse location.
