EXPERIMENT CONTEXT

What these numbers mean

Fly connections are fixed. Only the added group was trained using Laya as a teacher.

Pixels stimulate selected visual projection cells through an artificial mapping. This is a model based on a measured connectome, not a living fly brain or a validated model of natural vision.

Brightness means recorded activity, not importance or understanding. A drawn connection does not establish why the model chose an action.
