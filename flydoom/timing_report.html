From fixed time constants to measured timing

Real L1/L2 population-mean recordings supervise the existing visual graph. High luminance is used for fitting; low luminance is reserved for evaluation.

Conditional calibration. The fitted constants describe this model under assumed sensor kinetics. They are not uniquely identified intrinsic membrane constants. The recorded signal is voltage-sensitive ASAP2f fluorescence, not calibrated millivolts. No live Doom checkpoint is replaced.

Observation model & provenance

Retinal pulse → measured graph dynamics → assumed indicator lag → causal 8.333 ms averaging → positive observation gain. Only training records determine the gain; test records are neither shifted nor rescaled to improve agreement.
